# The claim

Brevity is the oldest advice in the trade [@strunk1918], and it has outlasted
most of the theories that came after it.

## Where it holds

Information theory gave the intuition a shape [@shannon1948]. Two sources
agreeing is worth more than either alone [@strunk1918; @knuth1984].

## Where the syntax must not fire

These are not citations and must survive to the page as the author wrote
them:

- An email address: jack@example.com
- A price: 10 @ £4 each
- A bracketed aside [see @strunk1918, p. 12], with a prefix and a locator
- An empty bracket [] and a bare footnote-looking thing [note]
- A ==mark to come back to== beside a real citation [@knuth1984]
