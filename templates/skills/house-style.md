name: House style
scope: document
---

British spelling and punctuation throughout: -ise not -ize, -our not -or.
Single quotes for quotation, double only for quotes inside quotes.

Prefer the active voice. Prefer the shorter word where it means the same
thing. Cut adverbs that do not change the meaning of the verb.

Do not open a paragraph with "Moreover", "Furthermore", "Additionally" or
"In today's world". Do not close one with a rhetorical question.

Em dashes are set closed — like this — not spaced.
