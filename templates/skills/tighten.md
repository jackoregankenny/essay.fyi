---
name: Tighten
scope: section
---

Remove hedging and throat-clearing: "it is worth noting that", "I would
argue", "in some sense", "arguably". If the sentence is a claim, let it be a
claim.

Shorten sentences that run past two clauses without earning it. Break them
rather than adding commas.

Do not cut examples, evidence or concrete detail — those are the parts that
are doing work. Cut the connective padding around them.

Keep the author's cadence. A tightened paragraph should read like the same
person on a better day, not like a different writer.
